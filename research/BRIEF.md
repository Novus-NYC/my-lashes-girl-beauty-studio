## Proposed direction (AGENTS.md steps 1–3, for discussion, not built)

**Three sentences.**
1. A calm, precise, appointment-only studio tucked on the 12th floor near Grand Central, where one skilled artist designs lashes for each eye shape.
2. Visitors are Midtown women, many Chinese-speaking, comparing styles and prices on their phones.
3. They should **pick a style, see the price and book on Square** in two taps, or ask about the mobile service.

**One idea: "the lash map."** Lash artists plan every set on a mapping diagram: an eye outline divided into zones with lengths (8–9–10–11–12 mm) and curl types. The client's whole pitch is "custom design for your eye shape", so the map becomes the visual system:
- Thin technical lines, millimetre annotations and zone numbers.
- Each style (Natural, Full, Anime, Hybrid, Volume) shown as its own map next to a real close-up photo.
- Typography pairs a precise, light Chinese/Latin serif with fine annotation lettering, like a technician's notes.
- Palette: one ink colour, a skin-tone paper and one accent. No pink-glitter salon clichés.

**Homepage sketch**
1. **Hero.** A huge close-up eye photo. The headline uses her words: "Custom lashes for your eye shape." The Chinese line comes first or alongside. Show Book and the 12th-floor address immediately.
2. **Styles.** A lash-map comparison of the five sets with prices and durations. This is the core tool, not a card grid.
3. **Refills.** A simple table for 2-week and 3-week refills (people come back for this).
4. **Process and aftercare.** Consult → map → apply. Aftercare only once the owner supplies it.
5. **Reviews.** A ClassPass 4.9 strip and 3 permitted quotes.
6. **Visit.** Hours, "next to Chipotle, 12th floor #1205", Grand Central directions and a mobile-service note.
7. **Also offered.** Facial, massage and scrub, small and secondary.
